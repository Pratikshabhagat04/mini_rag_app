COMPANY PROFILE: NimbusCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Overview
NimbusCart Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a mid-sized e-commerce technology company
founded in 2016 and headquartered in Pune, India. The company builds a
white-label online storefront platform used by small and medium retailers
across South Asia. As of the last internal report, NimbusCart employs 214
people across four offices: Pune (engineering HQ), Bengaluru (data science),
Mumbai (sales), and a small remote-first support team spread across Tier-2
cities.

Product Line
The core product is "CartOS", a modular storefront builder that lets a
merchant launch a fully functional online store in under an hour. CartOS is
organized into three tiers:

1. Starter Tier - Free for merchants with under 100 monthly orders. Includes
   a storefront theme editor, basic inventory tracking, and manual payment
   reconciliation.
2. Growth Tier - Priced at INR 2,499 per month. Adds automated payment
   gateway integration (Razorpay and PayU), abandoned-cart email recovery,
   and a basic analytics dashboard.
3. Scale Tier - Priced at INR 8,999 per month. Adds multi-warehouse
   inventory sync, a recommendation engine, and dedicated account support.

Founding Team
NimbusCart was founded by three co-founders: Ritika Deshmukh (CEO, previously
a product manager at an unnamed logistics startup), Aman Verma (CTO,
background in distributed systems), and Farah Sheikh (COO, background in
retail operations). The company raised a seed round of USD 1.2 million in
2017 from a consortium of angel investors, followed by a Series A of USD 6.5
million in 2019 led by a Mumbai-based venture fund.

Engineering Practices
The engineering team follows a two-week sprint cycle. All production
deployments require passing an automated test suite and a manual review from
at least one senior engineer. The backend is written primarily in Python
using FastAPI, with a PostgreSQL primary database and Redis for caching and
session storage. The frontend storefront themes are rendered using a custom
templating engine written in TypeScript. NimbusCart migrated its
infrastructure from a single-region deployment to a multi-region deployment
on AWS (Mumbai and Singapore regions) in early 2022 to reduce latency for
customers in Southeast Asia.

Customer Support Policy
NimbusCart offers email support to all tiers with a target first-response
time of 24 hours on the Starter tier, 8 hours on the Growth tier, and 2 hours
on the Scale tier. Phone support is available only to Scale tier customers,
Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM IST. There is no support available on
public holidays observed in Maharashtra.

Known Limitations (as of the last report)
CartOS does not yet support multi-currency checkout, which has been the
single most requested feature from merchants selling to international
customers. There is also no native mobile app for merchants; store
management is currently web-only, accessible through a responsive browser
interface. A dedicated Android management app is listed as "under
exploration" but has no committed release timeline.
